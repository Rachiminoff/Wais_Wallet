# 💼 Wais Wallet

**Wais Wallet** is a mobile personal finance application designed around the principles of the *envelope budgeting system*. It enables users to proactively allocate their income into purpose-driven categories—referred to as **Wais Pockets**—such as Rent, Groceries, Leisure, and Savings.

By structuring finances around intentional allocation rather than reactive spending, Wais Wallet helps users maintain clarity, improve discipline, and make more informed financial decisions.

---

## 📌 Overview

A common challenge in personal finance is not the lack of funds, but the lack of visibility and structure in how money is used. Wais Wallet addresses this by:

- Encouraging **intentional budgeting** at the moment income is received  
- Providing **clear allocation tracking** across categories  
- Reducing the likelihood of overspending through predefined limits  

This approach ensures that every unit of currency has a defined purpose.

---

## ✨ Features

### 📊 Dashboard
- Consolidated view of financial status  
- **Safe-to-spend balance** for unallocated funds  
- Real-time breakdown of remaining balances per pocket  
- Intuitive visualization of budget distribution  

---

### 🧾 Budget Planner
- Allocate income across customizable budget categories  
- Structured planning for recurring and variable expenses  
- Helps enforce spending discipline through predefined limits  

---

### 🎯 Savings Goals
- Create and manage multiple savings targets  
- Track progress with visual indicators  
- Encourages consistent saving behavior over time
